## Introduction

At UseGrant, we are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring the security of your
personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when
you use our services.

**TL;DR:** We do not sell your data. You can delete your account at any time, and when you do, we
erase your personal data from our systems. However, we may retain payment data for accounting and
legal compliance purposes.

## Information We Collect

When you sign up for a UseGrant account, you provide us with certain information, including:

- Email address

We are committed to data minimization and only collect information that is necessary to provide our
services. If we need to collect additional information in the future, we will update this Privacy
Policy to reflect any changes.

## Cookies

We do not set any tracking cookies. We only use a few functional cookies to keep you logged in and
to remember your app preferences.

## Third Party Services

We use the following third party services:

- Polar for payment processing. Please refer to their
  [Privacy Policy](https://polar.sh/legal/privacy) for more information.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your email address for the following purposes:

- To send you login links/codes and other relevant information for account security
- To contact you regarding your account
- For payment processing

## How We Protect Your Information

We take appropriate measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration,
and destruction. Additionally, we do not track you in any way.

We do not use passwords; instead, we use magic links or one-time codes for enhanced security.

## Data Retention

We will retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you
services. If you wish to delete your account, you can do so by contacting us at
[support@usegrant.com](mailto:support@usegrant.com) or by deleting your account in the app. When you
delete your account, we will delete(wipe) your personal information from our systems instantly.
However, we may retain the payment data.

Payment data is retained for accounting and legal compliance purposes and is securely managed by our
payment processor, Polar, in accordance with their Privacy Policy.

We may also delete your information on the following occasions:

- If required by law
- If you violate our [Terms of Service](https://usegrant.com/terms)

## Security Incidents

In the unlikely event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you
promptly in accordance with applicable laws and take immediate steps to secure your data and prevent
further unauthorized access.

**Note:** We do not support authentication methods that involve storing passwords or granting
persistent access to your account. Instead, we use magic links or one-time codes, which provide
temporary access and enhance your account security by ensuring your data remains protected.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other
operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. When we make material changes, we will notify you by
posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and sending an email to the address you provided.

## Contact Us

We take your privacy very seriously and implement appropriate measures to protect it. If you have
any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via support@usegrant.com

By using our services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
