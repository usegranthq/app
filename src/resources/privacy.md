## Introduction

At UseGrant, we are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our services.

**TL;DR:** We do not sell your data. You can delete your account at any time, and when you do, we completely erase your data. However, we may retain payment data for accounting purposes.

## Information We Collect

When you sign up for a UseGrant account, you provide us with certain information, including:

- Email address

We might also collect few more information in the future, when that happens we will update this Privacy Policy to reflect any changes.

## Cookies

We do not set any tracking cookies. We only use a few functional cookies to keep you logged in and to remember your app preferences.

## Third Party Services

We use the following third party services:

- Polar for payment processing. Please refer to their [Privacy Policy](https://polar.sh/legal/privacy) for more information.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your email address for the following purposes:

- To send you login links/codes and other relevant information for account security
- To contact you regarding your account
- For payment processing

## How We Protect Your Information

We take appropriate measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. Additionally, we do not track you in any way.

We do not use passwords; instead, we use magic links or one-time codes for enhanced security.

## Data Retention

We will retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. If you wish to delete your account, you can do so by contacting us at [support@usegrant.com](mailto:support@usegrant.com) or by deleting your account in the app. When you delete your account, we will delete(wipe) your personal information from our systems. However, we may retain payment data for accounting purposes.

Payment data is retained for accounting purposes and is securely managed by our payment processor, Polar, in accordance with their Privacy Policy.

We may also delete your information on the following occasions:

- If required by law
- If you violate our [Terms of Service](https://usegrant.com/terms)

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and sending an email to the address you provided.
